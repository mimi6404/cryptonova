Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange

Introduction

Diffie-Hellman is a key exchange algorithm.
It allows two parties to establish a shared secret over an insecure channel.

It is not encryption, but a method to generate a shared key.

Practice Lab

Diffie-Hellman Steps

1. Public values: p (prime), g (generator).

2. Alice chooses private a.

3. Bob chooses private b.

4. Alice computes A = g^a mod p.

5. Bob computes B = g^b mod p.

6. Shared Secret = B^a mod p = A^b mod p.

Even if someone knows p, g, A, B, they cannot easily compute the shared secret.
