Mixed (Keyword) Substitution Cipher

Introduction

Mixed substitution cipher uses a keyword to create a new
scrambled alphabet. The keyword letters come first,
then the remaining letters of the alphabet without repetition.

Example:
Keyword = KEY
PlainAlphabet= ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ
CipherAlphabet = KEYABCDFGHIJLMNOPQRSTUVWXZ
