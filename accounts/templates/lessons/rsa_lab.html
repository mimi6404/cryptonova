RSA Encryption (Asymmetric Algorithm)

Introduction

RSA is an asymmetric cryptographic algorithm.
It uses two keys: Public Key for encryption and Private Key for decryption.

Based on large prime numbers and modular arithmetic.

Practice Lab

RSA Algorithm Steps

1. Choose two large prime numbers (p, q).

2. Compute n = p × q.

3. Compute φ(n) = (p−1)(q−1).

4. Choose public exponent e.

5. Compute private exponent d.

Encryption: C = M^e mod n

Decryption: M = C^d mod n

RSA security depends on the difficulty of factoring large numbers.
