Twofish Encryption

Introduction

Twofish is a symmetric block cipher designed by Bruce Schneier in 1998.
It was a finalist in the AES competition.

• Block size: 128 bits
• Key size: 128, 192, or 256 bits
• 16 Feistel rounds
• Considered highly secure
