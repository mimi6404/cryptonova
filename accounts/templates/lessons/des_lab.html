DES Encryption (Data Encryption Standard)

Introduction

DES is a symmetric block cipher developed in 1977.
It uses a 56-bit secret key and works on 64-bit blocks.

DES performs 16 Feistel rounds.
Today, DES is considered insecure due to short key length.

DES Round Structure

1. Initial Permutation (IP): Rearranges bits.

2. 16 Feistel Rounds:

  • Expansion (32 → 48 bits)
  • Key Mixing (XOR)
  • S-Box Substitution
  • P-Permutation

3. Final Permutation (FP): Reverse of IP.

DES uses a Feistel structure where left and right halves are processed and swapped in each round.
