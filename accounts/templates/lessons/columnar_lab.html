Columnar Transposition Cipher

Introduction

Columnar Transposition is a transposition cipher.
The plaintext is written into rows under a keyword,
then columns are read according to alphabetical order of the keyword.

Example:
Plaintext = HELLOWORLD
Keyword = ZEBRA
Ciphertext = ODLREOLLHW
