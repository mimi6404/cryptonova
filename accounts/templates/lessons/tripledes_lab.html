3DES Encryption (Triple DES)

Introduction

3DES (Triple DES) is an improved version of DES.
It applies the DES algorithm three times to increase security.

Encryption pattern: Encrypt → Decrypt → Encrypt (EDE)

Practice Lab

3DES Structure

1. First DES encryption with Key1

2. DES decryption with Key2

3. DES encryption again with Key3

3DES increases security compared to DES, but is slower and now replaced by AES.
